DSV Integrations

Damian Spark Ventures LLC ("DSV") builds and operates custom data integrations for individual clients under signed service agreements. This page is the informational landing page for those integrations — it is not a public product or an app store listing.

Each integration connects to a single client's Intuit Enterprise Suite or QuickBooks Online company, with that client's authorization, to move their own accounting and dimension data into a data platform DSV builds and operates on their behalf.

See our privacy policy and end-user license agreement for details on how data is handled.
